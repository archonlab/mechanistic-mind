# UNDERCOVER

Undercover is a **manually controlled physical participant** in the same world as the cognitive Tiktaalik agents.

## Architecture

| Role | Physical? |
|------|-----------|
| Cognitive agent 0 | Yes — body |
| Cognitive agent 1 | Yes — body |
| Undercover controller | **No** — control path only |
| Undercover body | **Yes — exactly one** |

The controller is not another physical entity. Canonical identity is the Undercover / body-slot representation — not a duplicate `AGENT_2` + experimenter-body pair.

## What Undercover can and cannot do

**Can:** move/act through ordinary physical channels (locomotion, contact, optical presence, signals as the body affords).

**Cannot directly write:** cognition, memory, beliefs, predictions, or action selection of other agents.

Influence on other agents occurs only through world-mediated physics and sensing.

## Scientific purpose

Controlled physical perturbation while preserving agent-accessible information constraints.

Do not describe Undercover as “teaching” unless a specific experiment demonstrates an acquired behavioral difference under those constraints.

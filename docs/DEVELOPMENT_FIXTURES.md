# Development fixtures — ontology boundary

Classification: **`DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURE`**

## Purpose

Entities whose identifiers follow the `resource-*` pattern, and the resource mechanisms associated with them, are temporary development fixtures in Acanthostega Beta 4.0.

They exist so researchers can exercise and validate:

- physical contact and soft-body overlap;
- sensorimotor consequence paths;
- material transfer;
- work accounting;
- ecology-related mechanism wiring.

They are scaffolding for mechanism development, not a finished world ontology.

## Current physical and evidence authority

Within runs produced by this package:

- their physical effects are real in the simulation;
- those effects are recorded in scientific evidence when present;
- Analyzer reports and UI help mark them as development fixtures;
- analysis does **not** hide or exclude their effects.

Runtime labels used by saved evidence identity are unchanged. This document does not rewrite historical receipt field names.

## Prohibited interpretations

Do **not** treat `resource-*` entities as:

- pre-authored meaningful objects of the Mechanistic Mind world;
- food;
- rewards;
- goals;
- agents of intentional seeking;
- final ecological entities.

Contact with a development fixture is physical (and, when recorded, evidential). It is not reward, communication, recognition, or strategy.

## Future replacement

Removing or replacing these fixtures belongs to future work involving:

- Ecology;
- a spherical world;
- the Causality Generator.

Such replacement will change the public model. It will require:

- a new public-model fingerprint;
- scientific revalidation of affected claims.

## Relation to fingerprints

This fixture classification is a documentation and analysis boundary for the current stage. It does **not** by itself change that fingerprint. Changing the resource ontology in runtime would.

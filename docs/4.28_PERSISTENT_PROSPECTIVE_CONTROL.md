## Research question

Can a prospectively selected continuation remain causally relevant across multiple subsequent physical actions without being reconstructed and independently reselected from scratch at every motor primitive?

## Implementation

- Module: `mechanistic_mind/research/persistent_prospective_control.py`
- Reuses mismatch geometry from Update 4.16 `state_compatibility`
- Config (default OFF): `persistent_prospective_control`
- Ablations: persistence off; motor-chunk off
- Persistence rule: predictive support / mismatch — **not** `commit_for=N`
- Safety lifetime cap only (`MAX_TRACE_LIFETIME_TICKS` from 4.16) — not the scientific commitment rule
- Cognition: preferred action from active structure may continue selection without full PSC rebuild when support holds

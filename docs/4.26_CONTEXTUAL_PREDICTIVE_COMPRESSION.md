## Research question

Can repeated partially varying experiences become compressed into a stable higher-order predictive structure that can later be reactivated from incomplete or altered observations?

## Implementation

- Module: `mechanistic_mind/research/contextual_predictive_organization.py`
- Bridge: `mechanistic_mind/research/contextual_stack_bridge.py`
- Config (default OFF): `CognitionConfig.contextual_predictive_organization`
- Ablations: `contextual_predictive_organization_ablate`, `_shuffle`
- Cognition state key: `contextual_organization`
- Observer: compact summary via `observer_compact` / FULL public-view section

Higher-order contexts form from **co-activation of lower-level member tokens** derived from compressed / multi-scale structure IDs and quantized agent-accessible channels. No `x`/`y`/cell/region IDs enter cognition. Forbidden analyst tokens (`PLACE`, `MAP`, …) are audited out of agent-facing stores.

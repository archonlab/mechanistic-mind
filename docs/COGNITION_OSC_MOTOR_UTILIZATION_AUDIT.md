## Executive answer

Why narrow OSC use? Because `_pick_supported(..., prefer_none=True, explore_rate=0.08)` defaults frequency/amplitude/emit to NONE unless a side-channel prediction MATCH exists; ordinary short runs show only endogenous single-factor probes atop ~78% joint no-ops. The channel and assembly preserve diversity; cognition's side-channel policy barely exercises it.

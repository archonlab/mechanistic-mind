# SCIENTIFIC_BOUNDARIES

Conservative terminology for Mechanistic Mind 2.0 Public Beta 1.

## Allowed descriptions

- physical state transition
- body state
- physical sensor input
- anonymous exteroceptive channels (`exo_0` …)
- predictive structure
- prospective continuation
- scenario competition (PSC)
- physical signal coupling
- foreign-body optical exposure
- temporal association
- partial observation
- acquired behavioral difference **where an experiment actually demonstrates it**

## Not claimed by default

- recognition / identification of another agent
- understanding / semantic meaning
- communication / language
- teaching / imitation
- intention / desire / fear
- consciousness / subjective experience
- social cognition

## Observer vs agent information

**Observer ground truth ≠ cognition-accessible information.**

The UI and Analyzer may display world fields, identities, and diagnostics that agents never receive. Agent-accessible inputs remain the defined observation / exo channels and internal predictive state.

## Vision / Visual Forensics

- **BODY_OPTICAL_EXPOSURE** means measurable optical contribution in the physical composition path.
- It does **not** mean “recognized another agent.”
- Body-derived exo delta uses the existing counterfactual: final exo minus exo without foreign bodies, within actual optical semantics.
- Exposure threshold used in forensics: `foreign_body_total > 1e-12`.
- Cognition linkage from exposure to later action/selection remains **NOT_ESTABLISHED** unless a specific experiment proves otherwise.

## Signals

Physical signal fields and contacts are physical couplings. They are not, by themselves, evidence of communication.

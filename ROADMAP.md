# Mechanistic Mind — Research Roadmap

This document separates **what Acanthostega Beta 4.0 already provides** from **research directions that are not present in the current public model**.

It does not assign schedules, completion levels, or claimed future scientific outcomes.
It does not claim consciousness, understanding, intention, communication, recognition, goals, or strategy.

Related ontology boundary: [DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURES.md](DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURES.md) (classification **`DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURE`**).

---

## 1. CURRENT — Acanthostega Beta 4.0

Present in the current local final-candidate package:

- Local MM Observer with bundled runtime and zero-setup Linux launch.
- Acanthostega Beta 4.0 public model (fingerprint `3666b58d67932821`).
- Required Acanthostega mechanisms enabled by default for ordinary runs.
- Prospective Scenario Competition (PSC): starts OFF; scheduled ON from tick 1000.
- Dual first-person vision (FPV) with honest unavailable/missing evidence.
- Hearing playback with one master researcher playback-volume control.
- Read-only Analyzer over frozen evidence; Markdown and JSON export schema v2.
- Lifecycle-owned application window and automatic shutdown of the owned backend.
- `resource-*` markers classified as **`DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURE`** (retained; not canonical ontology).

Interpretation limits for the current model remain in force: receipts are not intention; optical occupancy is not recognition; physical signal coupling is not communication.

## 2. NEXT — immediate stabilization

Near-term work stays inside packaging, documentation honesty, Observer/Analyzer operability, and scientific-discipline hygiene — without changing the public-model fingerprint unless a deliberate scientific seam requires it.

Examples of stabilization scope (not a delivery schedule):

- Keep zero-setup launch and lifecycle shutdown reliable for ordinary users.
- Keep PSC, Dual FPV, Hearing, and Analyzer export behavior truthful and operable.
- Keep development-fixture wording consistent across README, Analyzer, and UI help.
- Preserve dirty-tree / local-candidate discipline until an explicit publish seam.

Stabilization is not Ecology replacement, not a new body ontology, and not a new public model.

## 3. RESEARCH DIRECTION — Ecology + spherical world + Causality Generator

Future research may replace temporary `resource-*` scaffolding with a coherent ecology on a spherical world, supported by a Causality Generator for authored causal structure.

Until that replacement seam lands:

- `resource-*` remains **`DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURE`**.
- Fixtures may continue to exert real physical effects inside current runs and remain in recorded evidence.
- They must not be treated as food, rewards, goals, or final ecological entities.

Replacement of this fixture layer is a public-model change. It requires a **new fingerprint** and **scientific revalidation**.

See [DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURES.md](DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURES.md).

## 4. RESEARCH DIRECTION — Body and morphology

Future body research may explore richer morphology and effector geometry, including:

- configurable manipulator / effector count;
- articulated morphology;
- support and balance;
- reachability;
- contact geometry;
- deformation;
- work accounting.

These are research directions, not present as a finished user-facing morphology product in Beta 4.0.
Any such expansion that changes the public model requires fingerprinting and revalidation discipline.

---

## 5. RESEARCH DIRECTION — Hearing and physical signals

Current Hearing remains a researcher playback / observability surface over physical and receptor evidence, with **one master playback-volume control**. Playback volume does not enter the simulation.

Future work may deepen physical-signal honesty (calibration, comparison views, offline reconstruction) without claiming semantic hearing or communication.

Physical signal coupling is not communication.

---

## 6. RESEARCH DIRECTION — Vision and researcher observation

Current Dual FPV and related Observer views show organism-accessible or researcher optical evidence with explicit unavailable states.

Future observation work may improve researcher geometry, alignment, and coverage honesty.
Optical occupancy is not recognition.

---

## 7. RESEARCH DIRECTION — Analyzer and scientific evidence

Current Analyzer is read-only over frozen snapshots; core chains and export completeness are distinct from full auxiliary-layer coverage.

Future Analyzer work may improve layered coverage, reconstruction honesty, and export clarity — still without replaying physics/cognition as a substitute for missing evidence, and without mutating saved runs.

Derived associations are not automatically causal. **NOT AVAILABLE** is distinct from zero.

---

## 8. Scientific release discipline

- Canonical model mechanisms for ordinary runs should be enabled automatically; users should not need a manual mechanism checklist for the normal set.
- Perception, Cognition, and Predictive must **not** become duplicate switch panels for controls already present in Ablations.
- Dedicated UI sections are justified only by distinct scientifically meaningful parameters or evidence-interpretation needs.
- Public-model changes require a new fingerprint and scientific revalidation.
- Local candidates may exist without publication; publication is a separate deliberate act.
- Do not present research directions as completed capabilities of the current package.

---

## 9. OUT OF SCOPE for current Beta 4.0

Not claimed by Acanthostega Beta 4.0:

- Canonical ecology replacing `resource-*` fixtures.
- Spherical-world ecology product.
- Causality Generator as a shipped authoring product.
- Finished articulated morphology / multi-effector body product.
- Semantic communication, recognition, intention, goals, or strategy.
- Consciousness, understanding, or biological equivalence.
- AppImage / published store distribution (unless a later release seam explicitly provides it).
- Automatic promotion of research directions into the current fingerprint.

`resource-*` fixtures remain present and classified as **`DEVELOPMENT_FIXTURE`** until the Ecology + spherical-world + Causality Generator replacement seam.
